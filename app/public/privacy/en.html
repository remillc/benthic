Updated on 19 November 2026

OpenDives is a free and open-source dive log (GNU GPL version 2), developed by Christian Rémillard. This page says which data the app uses, where it goes and how to erase it.

In short

  • There is no OpenDives account: the developer receives none of your data.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no trackers.
  • Your logbook stays on your device, and in your Subsurface cloud account if you use one.
  • Your photos stay on your device.

On your device

The logbook (dives, sites, equipment, notes), your settings and the references to your photos are stored in the app's private storage. Without an account, nothing else leaves it, except what "Online services" below describes.

With a Subsurface account

If you sign in to a Subsurface cloud account, or create one, your logbook is synced with the servers of the Subsurface project (ssrf-cloud-*.subsurface-divelog.org). This service is not run by OpenDives: its own terms apply. The logbook is stored there the way Subsurface stores it; photos are not sent, only their reference.

Your email address and password are kept on the device, encrypted by the system (Android Keystore), so that syncing does not ask for them again. They are only sent to the Subsurface servers.

Dive validation (OpenDives+)

When a buddy or an instructor validates a dive, their name, role, instructor number (if they give one) and signature are kept in your logbook, with the dive. With a QR code, the OpenDives+ server passes the confirmation along: the code, a summary of the dive (date, site, depth, duration) and the signer's name, role and instructor number stay there only until it is delivered, ten days at most, then are erased. A signature drawn on the screen is never sent to the server. You can remove a validation at any time: it is then erased from your logbook. There is no consent screen: the signer sees what is kept before signing or confirming.

Emergency sheet (OpenDives+)

The emergency sheet shows your name and, if you turned them on in "Sheet content", your date of birth, blood type, emergency contact, diving insurance and medical certificate. This information comes from your profile, kept encrypted with the rest of your OpenDives+ account. The sheet contains health data: only you can share it, as an image or a PDF, and the app reminds you before sending. OpenDives does not send it to anyone.

Online services

  • Maps: the base maps come from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), built from OpenStreetMap data. As with any website, this service sees your IP address and the area of the map shown. The maps of the sites in your logbook are prepared ahead, on Wi-Fi: this service therefore sees the area of each of these sites, even if you do not display it.
  • Country, region and city of a site: the position of a site is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) to find its country, region and city. Offline, a built-in, less precise map is used instead.
  • Searching for a place on the map: the name you search for is sent to Nominatim only when you start the search. A map area's name can also be suggested from its position, sent to the same service.
  • divelogs.de: only if you choose to import your divelogs.de logbook when creating an account. Your divelogs.de credentials are sent to divelogs.de for this import, then forgotten: they are never stored.

Permissions

  • Location: to centre the map on you and to place a site. It is only stored if you use it for a site. Android versions before 12 also require it to search for a dive computer over Bluetooth.
  • Bluetooth: to download the dives from your dive computer.
  • Internet: for syncing and the services above.

Reporting a problem

"Question / report a problem", in About, opens your email app with a message to support@opendives.app that contains technical details: OpenDives version, system and model of the device, language, units, with or without an account, number of dives, last sync and last error. You see this message and decide whether to send it.

Error reports

When the app catches an error or has crashed, it shows the report and asks "Send" or "Don't send". Nothing is sent without your tap, and there is no setting that sends reports on its own. If you tap "Send" without a connection, the report is kept on the device and offered again later.

A report contains: the OpenDives version and edition, the platform, system version and model of the device, the error message and its technical trace, the page of the app that was open (for example "/dive/42") and the last lines of the app's own log. Before it is shown, e-mail addresses, tokens, coordinates and user names in file paths are removed from it. It contains no dive, no site, no name, no buddy, no e-mail address, no account identifier and no position. It is sent to the OpenDives server without any account, kept for 180 days, then deleted.

Erasing your data

  • Uninstalling OpenDives erases everything it stored on the device.
  • "Delete account", in Account, deletes your account and your logbook from the Subsurface cloud.

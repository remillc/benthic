These terms apply to Benthic+, the Benthic subscription that keeps your logbook on the Benthic server. They are accepted once, when Benthic+ first starts, and again when they change. The privacy policy says which data the app uses and where it goes.

Your logbook is yours

Your dives, sites, equipment and documents remain yours. You can export your logbook at any time, in Subsurface's format, which other programs read too. You can cancel your subscription whenever you want: your logbook stays readable on your device.

Your data is encrypted

Your profile, your health data and your documents are kept encrypted on the Benthic server.

The shared map of sites

The sites in your logbook feed Benthic's shared base of dive sites, which Benthic+ subscribers see in the Sites tab ("Shared map"). There is no option to opt out, neither for the whole logbook nor site by site: it is a condition of Benthic+.

  • What is contributed, for each site you dived: its name, position, country, region, water type, the depth you usually log there, the number of your dives at this site and of those in the last 12 months.
  • What is never contributed: your notes and the site's description, your name and your buddies' names, your e-mail address, the dates of your dives. The base keeps no identifier of your account: nobody, not even the administrator, can tell which sites come from you.
  • A site without a position or without a dive is not contributed. A site you remove from your logbook leaves the base at the next send.
  • Duplicates are merged by the server, or by the administrator when the server cannot decide. A merge never changes your logbook: your site keeps its name and position.
  • A site of the base shows its number of dives, or "Fewer than 5 dives" under five. The number of logbooks is never shown in the app.
  • "Report" sends your report to the moderators of the shared map, without your name.
  • When you delete your account, your sites leave the base.

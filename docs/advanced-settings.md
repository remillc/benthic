# Advanced settings

A running list of the settings Christian considers advanced: settings most divers should never need to touch, which keep their place in Settings for now. Started on 12 November 2026. Nothing changes in the interface yet; the list is the input for a later decision on how to present them (a group of their own, hidden behind a switch, or the recreational mode of to-do #17).

| Setting | Where | Default | Core preference | Added | Why advanced |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Afficher les bouteilles inutilisées | Réglages › Équipement | on (unlike Subsurface, off) | `include_unused_tanks` | 12 November 2026 | Every cylinder entered by hand or imported is shown by default and the diver cleans up as needed. Turning it off hides the cylinders the core considers unused (no pressure drop, no gas switch to it, not the CCR oxygen), which also hides unbreathed bailout cylinders. |
| Photos sur cet appareil, Vidéos sur cet appareil | Réglages › Carnet (Benthic+ only) | photos: celles des 12 derniers mois; videos: à la demande | Benthic app settings (`mediaPhotos`, `mediaVideos`) | 23 November 2026 | Which originals of the synced photos and videos are downloaded on Wi-Fi: all, those of the last 12 months, on demand. Thumbnails are always downloaded. |
| Distance maximale d'une suggestion de site | Not in Settings yet: a constant in the code | 500 m (1 640 ft) | Benthic app setting (no core preference) | 14 November 2026 | On « Plongées sans site », a dive with a GPS position gets the nearest site as a suggestion only when that site is within this distance. Farther away, the row says that no site is close and « Nouveau site » creates one at the dive's position. Inbox task 148. |

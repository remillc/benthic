# Colour contrast audit of the mock-ups, 14 November 2026

Asked by Christian. Target: WCAG 2.2 AA, the level Benthic aims for (`docs/system-design.md`, section 3). Text needs 4.5:1, large text (24 px, or 18.66 px bold) 3:1. Disabled controls are exempt.

## Method

- **Scope:** all 412 boards of the canvas page « Maquettes », copies excluded. Each board was rendered in Chromium (Playwright).
- **Plain backgrounds:** for every visible text node, the text colour, with the opacity of its ancestors, was compared with the first opaque background behind it. Translucent layers were blended.
- **Gradients and images:** 285 texts sit on a gradient or an image, mostly the header band. Those boards were rendered a second time with all text made transparent, and the pixels behind each text were measured. The median ratio decides, and the worst pixel is noted.
- **Limits:**
  - SVG text (charts, map labels) was not measured.
  - Template placeholders (`{{…}}`) were measured with the colours they will have.
  - Texts under a modal scrim show low ratios on purpose. Those cases were set aside.

## Findings

### 1. Header band (the main problem)

The radial gradient is lightest at the top left (#A9C4E2, then #7199C2), right where the page title and the summary line sit.

| Text | Colour | Measured (median, worst) | Needed | Boards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Page title « Carnet », « Statistiques », « Outils », « Compte », « Sites » (22 px, 500) | #FFFFFF | 3.3 to 4.2, down to 2.8 | 4.5 | about 70 (every Android section page and the tablets) |
| Summary « 9 plongées · 4 sites · … » (14 px) | #D4E2F2 | 3.0 to 4.1, 2.6 on landscape tablets | 4.5 | about 50 |

Reference ratios on the stops: white on #7199C2 is 2.98, on #5584B5 3.92, on #4A78A8 4.62. #D4E2F2 on #3F6F9F is 4.00, on #2D5E90 5.12.

### 2. Account avatar on the band

The initial #114477 sits on a white pill at 90 %, which lets the band through. Measured 3.96 on the « Carnet, conflit » boards (a lighter band variant). It is fine elsewhere (8.7).

### 3. Plain backgrounds

| Where | Text | Colour on background | Ratio | Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tertiary banner (« attendre avant l’avion », suggested weight): key value in rust | « 7 h 40 » (13 px, 600) | #B4530F on #FBE3B2 | 4.00 | 4.5 |

Exempt, noted only: the disabled fields and buttons (opacity 0.38 and 0.6) of the sign-in and sign-out screens, and the Google Play rating dialog, which is the system's own UI.

## Corrections proposed

1. **Header band:** make the area behind the title and the summary dark enough.
   - Option A: move the light centre of the radial gradient off the text, from 38 % to about 75 %, to the right of the avatar, and start the band at #4A78A8 or darker under the text.
   - Option B: keep the gradient and put a faint dark veil (#114477 at about 35 %) behind the left half of the band only.
   - Either way, the summary #D4E2F2 needs a background at least as dark as #2D5E90, or the summary becomes #FFFFFF at 90 %.
   - To check: white ≥ 4.5 and summary ≥ 4.5 everywhere, including landscape tablets.
2. **Avatar:** an opaque white pill (#FFFFFF at 100 %).
3. **Rust value on the sand banner:** #9A4400 (5.2:1) instead of #B4530F, for this use only. The rust stays for graphics and large text.
4. **Error on the grey surface:** #B71C1C (5.0:1) on #E3E1DC, or put the error on white.
5. **Busy button:** label in #4B4A47 (6.4:1). Busy is not disabled.
6. **iPhone field labels:** #6C6C70 (5.2:1), the iOS secondary label colour, instead of #8E8E93.
7. **« Conservatisme VPM-B »:** if the row stays usable, #4A4D52. If it is disabled, mark it disabled (`aria-disabled`) so the exemption applies.

The high-contrast variant (task 149) does not replace these fixes: the normal mode must pass AA on its own.
